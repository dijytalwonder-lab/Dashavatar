================================================================================
 DASHAVATARA — WORLD 2: KURMA (The Churning of the Ocean)
 ART ASSET PROMPTS  —  CHIBI CUTE STYLE (match World 1)
================================================================================

World 2 currently runs on PROCEDURAL placeholder art (drawn in code in
src/systems/KurmaTextures.js). To swap in real art: generate transparent PNGs,
drop them in public/images/ under the SAME key, load them in PreloadScene.js —
the procedural version is then skipped automatically (guarded by texture key).

STYLE: identical chibi-cute look to World 1 — big eyes, rounded shapes, thick
clean outlines, soft shading, gold accents, deep-blue underwater palette. The
mood is the epic Samudra Manthan (churning of the ocean of milk).
