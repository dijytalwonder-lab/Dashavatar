================================================================================
 DASHAVATARA — CHAPTER 1: MATSYA
 ART ASSET GENERATION PROMPTS
================================================================================

The game currently runs on PROCEDURAL placeholder art (drawn in code, no image
files). To replace it with real art, generate the images below and drop them
into:   public/images/<name>.png

Then in src/scenes/PreloadScene.js, inside preload(), load each one under the
SAME key the game already uses, e.g.:

    this.load.image('matsya',   'images/matsya.png');
    this.load.image('enemyFish','images/enemyFish.png');
    ...

...and delete the matching block in src/systems/Textures.js so the real image
wins. Runtime keys must stay identical (listed in [brackets] below).

GLOBAL STYLE
- 2D mobile game, mythic Hindu art, painterly but clean, high contrast so
  sprites pop against a deep-blue underwater background.
- Consistent lighting from top. Soft rim light in cyan/gold.
- Transparent background (PNG with alpha). No drop shadows baked in.
- Palette: deep teal water (#041627 → #146c94), sacred gold (#ffd257),
  Matsya teal (#3fc1c9), demon purple (#8a2be2).

CHARACTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[matsya]  (~96x60, faces RIGHT)
A divine golden-teal fish, Vishnu's first avatar. Sleek body, elegant flowing
fins, a subtle golden halo/aura, one large expressive eye, a small gold crown
motif or tilak on the head. Heroic and graceful. Side view, swimming pose.

[boss]  (~220x220)  — Hayagriva, the demon
A menacing HORSE-HEADED demon rising from dark water. Elongated equine skull,
glowing red eyes, a mane of purple spectral flame, dark muscular torso dissolving
into shadow/water at the base. Holds a glowing bundle of sacred scrolls (the
stolen Vedas). Terrifying but mythic, not gory. Front view.

[sage]  (~64x64)
An elderly rishi (sage) with white beard, saffron/ochre robe, calm face, hands
in prayer, enclosed in a translucent rescue air-bubble. Gentle glow.
